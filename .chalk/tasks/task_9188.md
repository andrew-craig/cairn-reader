In AddLinkModal, the 'New items go to' Feed/Reads toggle shown when subscribing to a feed is too small, and it doesn't explain the choice: users can't tell what Feed vs Reads means for a subscription. Coordinate with the 'Add feed' option on the RSS page so both entry points share the same UI.
